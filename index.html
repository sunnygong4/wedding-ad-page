Common questions

Quick answers before you write.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as you can — summer weekends go first. That said, I keep a few short-notice dates open, so it is always worth asking even for a wedding a few weeks away.

Why are the rates this low?

Because this is my first wedding season, and I would rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise. I have years of experience photographing events, portraits, and documentary work — the founding rate is the honest trade: professional coverage at a price that reflects where my wedding portfolio is today.

Can we see wedding photos you have taken?

Not yet — that is exactly what the founding rate reflects. You can see how I photograph people, light, and real moments in my general portfolio, and the first couples who book this summer will set the wedding gallery.

When do we receive our photos?

A small preview set arrives within 72 hours. The full edited gallery is delivered as a polished online gallery, typically within three to four weeks depending on coverage length.

Do you travel outside Metro Vancouver?

Yes. Anywhere in Metro Vancouver is included. For weddings further out — Fraser Valley, Squamish, Whistler, the Island — send the location and I will quote a simple travel addition.

Can you cover tea ceremonies and multicultural weddings?

Happily. Tea ceremonies, banquets, religious ceremonies, and multi-part days are welcome — tell me the schedule and I will plan coverage around it.

How does booking work?

Send the date and location through the form below. If I am available, we will confirm the package, and a simple agreement plus a retainer holds your date. The balance is due around the wedding day.
